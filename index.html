What the run does

Each 2030 day borrows one complete 2015 day with the same weekday. Every source request keeps its category, minute and recorded coordinate. The orange lever changes only records inside the selected night window. One hundred repeats show how much the result depends on which historical days were drawn.

What it cannot see

  • Decibels, duration or unreported noise.
  • Caller identity, repeated callers or unequal access to 311.
  • Population change, new venues or policy change after 2015.
  • Why a sound becomes unacceptable.
  • The incident ZIP and MODZCTA boundary do not align perfectly: 32 source coordinates fall outside the polygon and are retained (derived by comparing the cited source files).
